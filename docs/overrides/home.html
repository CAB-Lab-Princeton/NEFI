{% include ".icons/nefi/mark.svg" %} Neural-Field Inversion · CAB Lab, Princeton University

Physics-faithful inverse problems, solved from a single measurement.

nefi recovers hidden fields such as spin densities, conductivities, sound speeds and thermal diffusivities from one measurement and a differentiable model of the instrument. No training data is needed. The unknown is represented by a coordinate neural field, the physics is enforced as a hard constraint, and an adaptive coarse-to-fine schedule fits the field to the data while built-in diagnostics report how far the result can be trusted.
